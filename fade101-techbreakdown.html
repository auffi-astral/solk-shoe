Fade 101

Technical Breakdown

1
Upper
Our upper leather has undergone a compost-capable tanning process. It's from a German tannery with robust environmental management techniques. The leather is chrome free and heavy metal free.
2
Laces & Webbings
These are made out of wood pulp from sustainably harvested eucalyptus, beech, and spruce trees. This yarn fibre has been certified for biodegradability and compostability in various environments.
3
Lace Tips
Also known as aglets, these tips are made from certified compostable material and attached by our own machine so that every lace is the right length and there's no production waste.
4
Glue
Our partially bio-based glue has been specially tested to fit our requirements for product durability and compost-capability. We make sure we only use what's essential.
5
Lining
The one-piece, zero-waste, knitted-to-shape lining is a SOLK developed blend of compostable yarns and plant fibres that are 100% biobased.
6
Midsole
Made to fade foamed materials that are as comfortable as they are fully compost-capable.
7
Outsole
Natural rubber quite literally slowly leaks out of a tree (like tree milk). It's the original rubber outsole material that coined the phrase sneakers.
